BIOGRAPHY

I am currently a graduate student and researcher in Engineering Physics, with a specialization in Biomedical Engineering at HCMUT - VNU-HCM. I completed the English-taught program in Engineering Physics at HCMUT (2021-2025) under the supervision of Assoc. Prof. Nguyen Thanh Nha and Assoc. Prof. Tran Trung Nghia.

RESEARCH INTERESTS

My research interests lie at the intersection of Computational Neuroscience, Numerical Analysis, Computational Physics and Artificial Intelligence (AI) in Biomedical Engineering. I focus on Biosignal: Electroencephalogram (EEG) & Electromyography (EMG), Brain-Computer Interface (BCI), Biomechanics, numerical methods in simulating mathematical models of soft tissues, bone remodeling algorithms, statistical modeling of neural codes and perceptual computation and decoding of human brain dynamics.

Besides my scientific research, I also assist in teaching lower-level courses. I enjoy reading, listening to music, and learning new things.

Objective: To seek PhD opportunities in advanced countries around the world and participate in collaborative scientific research with them.
