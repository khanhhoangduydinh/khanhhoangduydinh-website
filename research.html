RESEARCH

As a dedicated scientific researcher, my general interest lies in the broad areas of AI & Data Science for Health, Computational Neuroscience, Numerical Analysis in Biomedical Engineering and Biomechanics. More specifically, I am working on the following research topics: (1) Numerical methods in simulating mathematical models of soft tissues and bone remodeling algorithms; (2) Brain-Computer Interfaces (BCIs); (3) Machine learning (ML) and Deep Learning (DL) in biosignal; (4) Statistical modeling of neural codes. Some of my research projects are summarized below, and you can read the full list on the Publications page.

Bone Remodeling Simulations using Improved NNTRPIM

Bone remodeling is a complex biological process that can be modeled using advanced numerical methods. In this project, we investigate the development of high-fidelity biomechanical simulations using the Improved NNTRPIM approach. This method allows for more accurate predictions of bone density changes under various loading conditions, which have important use cases for orthopedic implant design and biomechanics [Paper1], [ISAS24].

Secure Brain-Computer Interfaces with Homomorphic Encryption

Brain-Computer Interfaces (BCIs) translate brain activity into computer commands, showing great promise in neurorehabilitation. However, processing sensitive neural data raises privacy concerns. We focus on enhancing the security of BCI systems using homomorphic encryption, specifically applied to Electroencephalogram (EEG) based P300 classification. The learned representations enable a wide variety of downstream applications while ensuring data privacy [Eureka25].
