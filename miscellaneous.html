READING

I spend a large amount of my free time reading. I enjoy reading about astrophysics, cosmology, science fiction, mystery thriller novel and light novels. Below are a few of my all-time favorite books:

  • "A Brief History of Time: From the Big Bang to Black Holes" - Stephen Hawking: 10/10 brilliant book. Did I understand every single aspect of it? Absolutely not. My brain simply doesn't have the RAM. I'd have to read it a dozen more times just to pretend I grasp all the interdisciplinary dots it connects.
  • "An Introduction to Modern Astrophysics" - Bradley W. Carroll & Dale A. Ostlie: I affectionately call it 'the orange book.' A friend in the US recommended it when I wanted to dip my toes into astrophysics, and apparently, it's the ultimate bedside bible for students in the field. It is a completely brilliant book.
  • "The Da Vinci Code" - Dan Brown: It's a total masterpiece of the thriller genre! Dan Brown really struck gold with the formula for that book. The way he blends art history, cryptography, and secret societies into such a high-stakes, 24-hour treasure hunt is incredibly gripping.
  • "Heartstopper" - Alice Oseman: This graphic novel turned my heart into absolute mush, and don't even get me started on the Netflix series! It's so rare for a story to have me in such a chokehold, constantly stalking the internet for the next plot update.

You can find my complete and frequently updated reading list on my Goodreads profile.
