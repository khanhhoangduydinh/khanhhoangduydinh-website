TEACHING EXPERIENCE

PH1001 General Physics I (Mechanics and Thermodynamics)

Year(s) taught: Since AY 2023/24

This is an introductory course in physics. It lays the mathematical foundation to prepare the students for engineering courses and their applications. The main topics covered in this course include kinematics, Newton's laws of motion, work and energy, momentum, rotational motion, and basic thermodynamics. Thus, the course will require both the understanding and development of theoretical results, as well as hands-on experience on practical applications.

PH1002 General Physics II (Electromagnetism and Optics)

Year(s) taught: Since AY 2024/25

This course focuses on physics in the context of electromagnetism and optics. We are primarily interested in electric fields, magnetic fields, Maxwell's equations, and the wave nature of light. This course will prepare you with the fundamental analysis skills to process and analyze electrical circuits and optical systems, in order to reveal valuable insights for making better decisions in biomedical engineering applications.
